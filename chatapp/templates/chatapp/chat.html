What are the main challenges of implementing RAG systems in real-world applications?
2:14 PM

The main challenges of implementing RAG systems in real-world applications include:

  1. Retrieval Quality – Finding the most relevant and up-to-date information from large document collections can be difficult, especially with domain-specific or technical content.
  2. Context Window Limitations – LLMs have limited context windows, which can lead to loss of important information from retrieved documents.
  3. Hallucinations – Despite using external documents, models may still generate incorrect or unsupported information.
  4. Latency and Cost – Retrieving and processing external data adds computational overhead, increasing response time and cost.
  5. Evaluation and Metrics – Measuring the effectiveness of RAG systems is complex, as traditional metrics for LLMs do not fully capture retrieval and grounding quality.
