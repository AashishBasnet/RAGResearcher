How it works

From a pile of PDFs to a conversation

1

Create a project

Keep each topic or thesis chapter in its own project, with its own papers and its own conversation.

2

Upload your PDFs

Papers are read, split and indexed automatically, so they are ready to question as soon as they finish.

3

Ask and verify

Answers cite the paper and page, and every source opens the PDF at that exact page.
